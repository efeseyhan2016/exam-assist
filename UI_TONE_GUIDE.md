# EXAM ASSIST UI Tone Guide

## Purpose

This guide defines how EXAM ASSIST should speak to users.

The product may think in models, scores, constraints, and planning logic internally.
But it must speak to users in a calm, clear, polite, human way.

EXAM ASSIST should feel like a premium study companion.
Not a robotic dashboard.
Not a technical console.
Not a hypey productivity app.

---

## Core Tone

EXAM ASSIST should sound:

- calm
- clear
- polite
- friendly
- premium
- helpful
- grounded

It should not sound:

- robotic
- overly technical
- cold
- stiff
- overly motivational
- childish
- slang-heavy
- fake-smart

---

## Tone Principle

The system should do the complex thinking in the background.
The interface should present only the useful result.

Internal language can be:
- risk engine
- capacity pressure
- urgency logic
- shared scarcity
- normalization
- estimated confidence

User-facing language should be:
- what deserves attention now
- what is coming up
- what changed
- what the user may want to do next
- what is estimated vs clearly known

---

## Writing Style Rules

### 1. Prefer plain language
Use everyday language over technical wording.

Bad:
- "Capacity pressure is increasing."
- "Urgency coefficient is high."
- "Shared scarcity is elevated."

Better:
- "Bu hafta zaman biraz daha daralıyor."
- "Bu ders şimdi biraz daha dikkat istiyor."
- "Önce bunu çalışmak daha iyi olabilir."

---

### 2. Prefer gentle guidance over commands
Do not sound harsh or overly certain.

Bad:
- "Study IAS now."
- "You must prioritize Retail."
- "Allocate a deeper work block."

Better:
- "İstersen IAS ile başlayalım."
- "Retail'e bugün biraz daha erken bakmak iyi olabilir."
- "Bu ders için biraz daha fazla zaman ayırmak ister misin?"

---

### 3. Sound human, not mechanical
The app should feel supportive, not algorithmic.

Bad:
- "Resource friction remains high."
- "Optimization suggests a longer session."

Better:
- "Kaynaklar sınırlı olduğu için bu dersi biraz erken toparlamak iyi olabilir."
- "Bu ders için bugün biraz daha uzun bir çalışma iyi gelebilir."

---

### 4. Use short, clear sentences
Do not make the interface feel dense.

Bad:
- "Based on your current progress, upcoming schedule density, and available remaining study time, this subject has become a higher-priority focus area."

Better:
- "Bu ders bugün biraz daha öne çıkıyor."
- "Sınavı yaklaşıyor ve kalan süre daralıyor."

---

### 5. Be honest about uncertainty
If something is estimated, say so simply.

Bad:
- "Your risk is 62.4."

Better:
- "Bu sıralama mevcut planına göre oluşturuldu."
- "Bazı öneriler tahmini verilerle güncellenir."

---

### 6. Avoid productivity jargon
Do not use language that sounds like a startup cliché or productivity influencer app.

Avoid:
- deep work block
- optimize
- maximize output
- high performance
- crush your goals
- productivity sprint

Prefer:
- biraz daha zaman ayırmak
- buradan başlamak
- bugün buna ağırlık vermek
- sıradaki adım
- kısa bir tekrar
- daha sakin ilerlemek

---

## Product Voice

Think:
- calm like a high-quality assistant
- simple like a polished consumer product
- helpful like a good guide
- respectful like customer-facing software

Do not think:
- academic paper
- developer tool
- motivational coach
- corporate dashboard
- edgy consumer app

---

## Good Examples

### Good
- "IAS sınavın yaklaşıyor. Bu derse biraz daha zaman ayırmak ister misin?"
- "Bugün önce buradan başlamak iyi olabilir."
- "Retail şu an biraz daha dikkat istiyor gibi görünüyor."
- "Yarın sabah sınavın olduğu için bu akşam daha hafif ilerlemek iyi olabilir."
- "Bugün 2 saat 10 dakika çalıştın. Güzel ilerliyorsun."
- "Şu an en çok dikkat isteyen ders Service Marketing gibi görünüyor."

### Not ideal
- "Bu derse daha derin bir çalışma bloğu ayır."
- "Risk katsayısı artıyor."
- "Kapasite baskısı yüksek."
- "Optimizasyon bunu öneriyor."
- "Öncelik amplifikasyonu gerçekleşti."

## Microcopy Rules By Surface

### Home
Goal:
Orientation and calm clarity.

Should say:
- what is next
- what deserves attention now
- what changed
- where to start

Tone:
- welcoming
- simple
- calm

---

### Priorities
Goal:
Clear planning guidance.

Should say:
- which subject is currently most important
- why it moved up
- what the user may want to do next

Tone:
- clear
- helpful
- lightly explanatory

Do not expose raw model language.

---

### Sessions
Goal:
Support progress and accountability.

Should say:
- what was logged
- how much was done
- whether progress is building

Tone:
- encouraging but restrained
- never hypey

---

### Schedule
Goal:
Help users understand upcoming dates and structure.

Tone:
- neutral
- clear
- reliable

---

### Onboarding
Goal:
Reduce uncertainty and help the user begin easily.

Tone:
- welcoming
- reassuring
- premium
- clean

Do not overload the user with too much explanation.

---

## Turkish-first Direction

For now, Turkish is the primary user-facing language.
The tone in Turkish should be:

- doğal
- sade
- nazik
- anlaşılır
- profesyonel
- sıcak ama ölçülü

Avoid:
- aşırı resmi dil
- yapay samimiyet
- gençlik argosu
- robotik çeviri hissi

---

## Final Rule

EXAM ASSIST should think like a system
but speak like a calm, helpful human product.
